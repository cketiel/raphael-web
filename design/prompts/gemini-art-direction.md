## Cómo trabajar
- Los prompts van **en inglés**, con el mínimo de texto dentro de la imagen.
- Genera cada prompt 2 o 3 veces y ajusta conversando («same image, calmer colors»).
- Adjunta en G1, G2 y G4 el logo, `apps/booking/public/brand/raphael-mark.png`: es el punto de partida de la marca.
- Elige de 2 a 4 imágenes y guárdalas en `design/proposals/art-direction/AAAA-MM-DD/` con un `notes.md` que diga por qué.
- **Esas mismas imágenes se adjuntan después a Stitch y a Claude Design**, en las dos plataformas.

## G1 · Moodboard general
> 🇪🇸 El ambiente: azul océano de la marca, calma clínica, luz de mañana en Miami y orden de un buen panel de control.

```text
A design moodboard for "Raphael", a web portal where dialysis centers and hospitals book non-emergency medical transport for their patients and follow every trip live. A calm, organized collage of: ocean-blue and teal color swatches starting from the attached pin logo (#005070 to #50a0c0) with warm sand and soft white accents; early-morning Miami light on a clean clinic reception desk; a wheelchair-accessible van at the curb, seen calmly, no sirens; a paper route map with a red start pin and a blue destination pin; precise dashboard details, rounded cards, subtle grid paper; humanist sans-serif type specimens. Mood: calm, trustworthy, precise, quietly warm. It should feel like healthcare logistics, not a ride-hailing app and not an emergency. No readable text.
```

## G2 · El menú lateral en tres estilos
> 🇪🇸 La seña de identidad del marco: el mismo menú lateral resuelto de tres formas.

```text
A product design exploration sheet showing three vertical web app sidebars side by side, each 260 px wide, for the "Raphael" medical transport booking portal, using the attached pin logo at the top. Each sidebar has four navigation items with simple line icons (calendar for Trips, buildings for Catalog, bell for Notifications with a small red counter "3", shield for Admin) and the first item active. Style 1: deep ocean-blue background (#0b2f45) with a soft teal glow behind the logo and a luminous left accent bar on the active item. Style 2: light frosted white sidebar with a thin ocean-blue border and a filled pill for the active item. Style 3: rich gradient from #005070 to #1f6f99 with a subtle topographic map texture and an organization card at the bottom. Clean light background, soft studio lighting, high detail. The only text is "Raphael", "Trips", "Catalog", "Notifications", "Admin" and "3".
```

## G3 · Estilo de ilustración de los estados vacíos
> 🇪🇸 Cómo se dibujan los estados vacíos («no hay viajes en estas fechas», «no hay avisos»).

```text
Three square cards showing the same empty-state illustration, "an empty calendar day with a small accessible van waiting calmly by a clinic", in three illustration styles: (1) minimal line art in ocean blue with a single teal accent; (2) soft flat vector shapes with gentle shadows in blue, teal and sand; (3) isometric miniature scene with rounded forms and pastel blue tones. Light background, generous white space, friendly and calm, nothing alarming. No text.
```

## G4 · La pieza protagonista: el día de una clínica
> 🇪🇸 La lista de viajes del día como póster: horas, pacientes, estados de colores y la ruta roja → azul.

```text
An elegant infographic poster of one day of patient transport for a dialysis center: a vertical timeline from 6:00 to 16:00 with ten trip cards placed at their pickup times, each card showing a small red pickup pin, a blue destination pin and a colored status dot (blue scheduled, green assigned, violet in progress, gray finished, red canceled), with one card highlighted as "in progress" and a tiny van moving between the pins. Ocean-blue and teal palette from the attached logo on a soft white background, refined typography, museum-quality information design. Only these numbers as text: "06:15", "08:45", "09:20", "12:30", "15:30".
```

## G5 · (Opcional) La página de Viajes en PC y en el teléfono
> 🇪🇸 Una primera intuición de la composición, en los dos tamaños a la vez.

```text
A realistic mockup of a laptop and a smartphone side by side, both showing the "Trips" page of an elegant medical transport booking portal: on the laptop, a deep ocean-blue sidebar with the attached pin logo, four summary cards and a clean table of trips with times, patient names, red and blue route pins and colored status labels; on the phone, the same trips as stacked cards with a bottom navigation bar. Calm ocean-blue and teal palette, soft daylight, professional and warm. Minimal text.
```
